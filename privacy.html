Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

CreativeVox Media ("we", "us") is a digital services business based in Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh, India. This page explains what information this website handles and how we use it. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

Information you share with us

When you use the enquiry form, email us, call us or message us on WhatsApp, you may share your name, email address, phone number, business name, the service you need, a timeline and the project details you write. We use this only to reply to you, discuss your project and send a quote.

How the enquiry form works

The form on this website does not send your details to a server or store them on the site. When you submit it, your own email app opens with a ready-to-send message. Nothing is sent to us until you press send in your email app. Once we receive your email, it is handled like any other email we receive.

Free Web Studio

The Free Web Studio planner runs in your browser. Your answers are saved only in your own browser so you can continue later, and are sent to us only if you choose to email or message us your brief. We do not store them on our servers.

Theme preference

The website saves your light or dark theme choice in your browser's local storage so it can remember it on your next visit. This stays on your device. You can clear it any time from your browser settings.

Third-party links

This website links to other services such as Instagram, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, the Udyam registration portal, and the live websites in our work section. Those services have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for how they handle information. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and Vercel, which may process basic technical data such as IP address to deliver pages.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with anyone except where needed to do the work you asked for, or where the law requires it. We keep enquiry emails only as long as needed to respond to you and to keep a record of our work together.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information of yours we hold, to correct it, or to delete it. Write to us at the email below and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Terms

Use of this website is also covered by our Terms & Conditions.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the website handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
