# CreativeVox Media — Product Context

## Purpose
CreativeVox Media is a digital services agency helping startups, small and local businesses, brands, creators, and influencers improve their online presence and turn ideas into practical digital experiences.

## Core services
- Website development and website / landing-page design
- Social media management and marketing
- Creative post and poster design
- Content creation
- Reel and video editing, including AI-generated and real footage
- Performance marketing and campaign support

Social media marketing should be presented as a connected service that includes social media management, creative post designs, and content creation—not as separate competing cards.

## Brand
- Tagline: “Your ideas. Our creative execution.”
- Process: Discover → Plan → Create → Refine
- Brand values: purpose-led creativity, business-first thinking, modern digital solutions, collaborative execution
- Current visual identity: red, near-black, white, with restrained warm accents
- Tone: clear, capable, modern, direct, and business-focused

## Primary conversion goals
1. Generate qualified project enquiries.
2. Help prospects quickly understand services and outcomes.
3. Show real portfolio work without inventing client results or performance claims.
4. Make contact options easy to use on mobile and desktop.
