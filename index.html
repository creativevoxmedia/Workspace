      THE CREATIVEVOX APPROACH
      A little about us

      Creative thinking.
      Real-world solutions.

      CreativeVox Media is a digital services agency in Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh, India. We build websites, run performance marketing, and edit video for businesses, startups and individuals.

      Who is behind it. CreativeVox Media is led by Fazil Ali (Founder) together with Adeeba Faiz (Executive Officer). We are a small, hands-on team, so the people you talk to are the people who do the work.

      How it started. It began in Bareilly with a simple observation: many good people and small businesses have real talent, but their online presence does not show it. We started by building personal portfolio websites, first for ourselves and the people around us, and grew from there into business websites, performance marketing and video editing.

      How we work. Understand the goal, create with purpose, and deliver work that is clear and consistent with your brand. No jargon, no unnecessary steps, and you always know what is happening with your project.
